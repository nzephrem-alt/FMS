About ISTA FORTIS

A dynamic construction company established in 2026 in Gikondo, Kigali, committed to excellence, innovation and reliability across all its operations.

Our vision

To become a leading and trusted construction and design company in Rwanda and across the region…, recognized for delivering affordable, innovative, durable, and high-quality infrastructure and interior solutions.

About us

ISTA FORTIS Group Ltd delivers high-quality services while building long-term trust with clients and partners…. The name reflects our core values: ISTA, representing vision and strategic advancement, and FORTIS, symbolizing resilience, courage and excellence. These principles guide every project and define our approach to service delivery.

Our mission

To provide reliable construction engineering, high-quality concrete products, and creative interior design solutions… that meet client needs, exceed expectations, and contribute to sustainable development, while building long-term relationships based on trust, professionalism, and excellence.

How We Work

A clear ten-step project management flow keeps every project on time, on budget and to standard.

01

Acquisition

Site visit, requirements, proposal, agreement and advance payment.

02

Setup

Client and project details, contract, payment schedule and deadlines.

03

Planning & budget

Phases, budgets, materials, labour, equipment and timelines approved.

04

Procurement

Compare quotations, approve, purchase and deliver materials to site.

05

Execution

Supervised daily work, safety compliance and a daily report by 9:00 AM.

06

Cost tracking

Every expense recorded and compared against the planned budget.

07

Payments

Payment schedule followed up; amounts received and balances tracked.

08

Quality control

Regular inspections, standards and safety checks, corrective actions.

09

Meetings

Bi-weekly reviews of progress, finances, challenges and deadlines.

10

Handover

Final inspection, issues resolved, handover and documents archived.
